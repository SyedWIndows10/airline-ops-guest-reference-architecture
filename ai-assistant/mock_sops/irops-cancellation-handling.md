# SOP 4.2 — Cancellation Handling

## Section 4.2.1 — Triggering conditions
A flight is cancelled when the reaccommodation-engine cannot produce a viable recovery within the MAJOR delay threshold, or when engineering-defect-linker flags a defect with no available replacement aircraft.

## Section 4.2.2 — Reaccommodation priority
All affected guests must receive at least one rebooking option within 30 minutes of cancellation classification. Guests holding connecting itineraries are prioritized ahead of point-to-point guests.

## Section 4.2.3 — Compensation
Compensation eligibility (meal vouchers, hotel, EU261-style compensation where applicable) is determined by the Guest Services platform and is out of scope for the reaccommodation-engine itself.
