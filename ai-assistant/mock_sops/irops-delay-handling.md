# SOP 4.1 — Delay Handling

## Section 4.1.1 — Classification
A delay is classified as MINOR (<60 min), MODERATE (60-179 min), or MAJOR (180+ min) based on estimated delay at time of disruption detection.

## Section 4.1.2 — Crew notification
For MODERATE and MAJOR delays, the crew-impact-evaluator must run a legality check before the flight is released. An AT_RISK or ILLEGAL_REQUIRES_REPLACEMENT result must be reviewed by a duty manager before departure.

## Section 4.1.3 — Guest notification timing
Guests should be notified of a MODERATE or MAJOR delay within 15 minutes of classification, via the guest-translation-layer and notification-dispatcher pipeline.
