# SOP 4.3 — Mechanical Defect Handling

## Section 4.3.1 — MEL correlation
Every MECHANICAL disruption event must be correlated against open Minimum Equipment List (MEL) entries for the affected tail number before a recovery plan is finalized.

## Section 4.3.2 — Engineering sign-off
A flight with an open MEL item affecting dispatch may not be released until engineering sign-off is recorded against the correlated defect record.

## Section 4.3.3 — Escalation
If no replacement aircraft is available within the region within 4 hours of a MECHANICAL disruption, the event escalates to the duty manager for manual recovery planning.
